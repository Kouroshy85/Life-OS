Life OS 1.8.0 reorganizes Finance around Overview, Transactions, and Fixed Income & Expenses.

- Add one-time or recurring income and expenses from the redesigned Finance screens. Missed recurring income is recorded once on reopening, and fixed expenses affect balances when payment is confirmed.
- Open Finance directly from the redesigned mobile bottom bar, alongside New goal, Home, and Week.
- Persian RTL and Jalali dates are defaults for new installations. Settings can switch between Jalali and Gregorian display. Existing transaction dates and amounts are preserved by the SQLite migration.
- Amounts use English digits and periods between groups of three, for example `300.000 تومان`. Rials remain the default and can be displayed as tomans.
- Use the free [iPhone web app](https://kouroshy85.github.io/Life-OS/) in Safari or add it to your Home Screen. Open it once while online to prepare it for offline use.

This release provides the Windows installer and a web ZIP. Back up your data from Settings before upgrading.
